## 6. How the business works

The supplied business profile describes the following general customer
workflow. This is an explanation of the business process, not a promise
that every job follows exactly the same steps.

1.  **Customer inquiry:** A customer contacts or visits the business
    with a new pump requirement, an installation need or an existing
    pump problem.
2.  **Understanding the requirement:** The business gathers relevant
    information about the site, borewell, water requirement and
    available electrical supply.
3.  **Product selection:** Suitable pump and accessory options are
    considered based on the customer's requirement.
4.  **Product and estimate discussion:** Available product and brand
    options are discussed with the customer.
5.  **Supply and installation:** Where installation is required, the
    relevant pump, pipes, cables and accessories are taken to the site
    and installed according to the agreed scope.
6.  **Testing:** The installed equipment is checked and operated to
    assess whether it is functioning as expected.
7.  **Handover and further support:** The customer receives the
    equipment or completed service, with further support discussed as
    applicable.
